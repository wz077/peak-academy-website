My Story

At the beginning of Year 12, I found myself falling behind in several subjects. I still remember receiving a mark of 35% on my first English assessment. It was a result I had never experienced before, and it forced me to take a step back and reconsider how I approached learning.

I realised that many of the study habits I had relied on were simply not effective. From that point on, I committed myself to improving not only how much I studied, but how I studied. Through consistency, better strategies, and a growing belief in my own abilities, I gradually transformed my results and entered every exam with confidence.

Today, as a Commerce and Computer Science student at UNSW, I am passionate about helping students build that same confidence.

Why Choose me?

I know first-hand that a disappointing result does not define a student's potential. Through personalised lessons, effective study strategies, and a strong focus on confidence, I help students overcome academic setbacks and achieve meaningful progress. Every student learns differently, which is why lessons are tailored to their individual needs, strengths, and goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are lessons offered online or in person?

Lessons are primarily conducted online. However, in-person arrangements can be made depending on location and availability.

How long are the lessons?

In one-on-one classes, lesson length can be chosen based on the student's needs. In group classes, lessons usually range from 1 to 2 hours.

How do group classes work?

Group classes consist of 2–4 students in the same year level and school, helping students prepare for similar topics and assessments.

Do you provide homework and resources?

Yes. Resources include guided homework, custom-made exams and OneNote topic explanations students can review at any time.

Do classes run during the holidays?

Yes. Classes continue within school holidays which help the student move ahead of their peers or prepare themselves for the next term by working on weaknesses.
